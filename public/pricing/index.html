Pricing questions

How is my price set?

By vehicle size and honest condition. A commuter sedan and a work truck full of dog hair are different jobs. Heavy pet hair, sand, spills or years of buildup add time. Anthony confirms your exact number from your photos before booking, and the price you're told is the price you pay.

Do you charge extra to drive to me?

There are no travel fees anywhere in our listed service areas, which include Casa Grande, Maricopa, Eloy, Coolidge, Florence, Arizona City, San Tan Valley and Queen Creek.

How do I pay?

After the walk-around, once you're happy. Cash, card, or the usual apps. Never a deposit, never prepayment.

Is there a discount for regulars?

Yes. Maintenance customers on a monthly or every-other-month rotation save on every visit and get priority scheduling. Ask Anthony when you book.

Gift certificates?

Absolutely, a detail makes a great gift. Text (520) 840-2452 and Anthony will set one up for any service or amount.
